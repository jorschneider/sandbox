BLACK & WHITE

Walk the gray valley as a small god. Look upon a thing, hold V, and speak — “emerald”, “the river is turquoise”, “make the roofs dark red” — and the color will ripple outward to everything of its kind, until the world is full.

best with a microphone · voice works in Chrome & Safari · no mic? press T and type your decrees
