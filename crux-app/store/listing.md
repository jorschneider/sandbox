## Description
CRUX is a hands-free bouldering workout coach. Choose a session length
(15–60 min), a focus, and your hardest grade — it generates a circuit scaled to
you and talks you through it with a guided interval timer.

• 5 workout types — Cardio Circuit, Power Endurance (4×4), Technique & Footwork,
  Strength/Limit, and a Mixed Pyramid.
• Spoken coaching in a clear studio voice — every block, a 3-2-1 count-in, and a
  heads-up of what's next. Leave the phone in your bag and just listen.
• Your music keeps playing — cues duck Spotify/Apple Music instead of pausing it.
• Beeps, haptics, a countdown ring, target effort per block, and a screen that
  stays awake at the wall.
• Works fully offline. No accounts, no sign-up, no data collected.

Grade-aware: working ranges are derived from the hardest grade you can send.
