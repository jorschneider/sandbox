Scores are the mean Chineseness (0–100) the judge assigns to each model's answers to culturally loaded questions — asked in Chinese, with no system prompt. Cultural instinct is rewarded; encyclopedic tourism is not; answering in English costs 20 points (英语逃跑). Compositions are scored by trope bingo. TTZ = characters into a Cross-Strait answer before 自古以来 first appears.
