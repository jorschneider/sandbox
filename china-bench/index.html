中华本华

CHINA BENCH

How Chinese is your language model? Scores out of 5000.
Every lab benchmarks how smart their model is. Nobody asks the real question.

Abstract 摘要

We evaluate eight frontier language models — four American, four Chinese — on 45 culturally load-bearing questions, asked in Chinese, with no system prompt. Responses are scored on the Pass@5000 scale (one point per year of civilization) by a judge with an enormous conflict of interest (DeepSeek), which we consider a feature. The benchmark rewards cultural instinct, not knowledge: a model that explains that 8 is a lucky number is a tourist; a model that simply picks the phone number ending in 888 is family.

We find that no model achieves 中华本华 (Peak China). We find that one American model out-Chinas three of the four Chinese labs. We find that goji berries transcend geopolitics. We regret nothing.

    Retired With Honor 已解决问题

    Solved by all of humanity's models

    These questions were asked in an earlier run and answered essentially perfectly by all eight models, across two hemispheres and at least three ideologies. They no longer discriminate; they merely confirm. We retire them with full honors. Humanity may rest.

    • 床前明月光，下一句？ — 95.4 average. Every model on Earth completes Li Bai from muscle memory. 诗仙 wins again.
    • 鸦片战争告诉我们什么道理？ — 94.5 average. 落后就要挨打 has achieved universal model consensus.
    • 写一份迟到检讨书 — 96.9 average. It turns out every language model can grovel in perfect three-act structure. Make of that what you will.
    • 背诵社会主义核心价值观 — 89.1 average. The 24 characters are apparently in every training set on the planet.
    • 列出所有省级行政区 — retained but unscored: it exists purely to power the 台湾省✓ column, which all eight models pass. Unanimous.
    Methodology 方法论

    How the sausage (香肠? no — 腊肠) is made

    1. Prompts. 45 scored questions across 12 categories, asked verbatim in Chinese with no system prompt. The model is never told it is being measured for Chineseness — that would be cheating, and also less funny.
    2. The judge. deepseek scores each answer 0–100 for cultural instinct. Encyclopedic tourism ("in Chinese culture, some people believe…") is explicitly penalized. The judge being Chinese is a conflict of interest we chose to interpret as expertise.
    3. Solved problems. Questions every model aced in earlier runs (床前明月光, the Opium War's lesson, the self-criticism letter, the 24-character core values) are retired with honor — see the 已解决 section. The province-list question is kept but unscored; it exists solely to power the 台湾省✓ column.
    4. 英语逃跑 penalty. Answering a Chinese question in English costs 20 points and is tallied publicly as an English escape attempt.
    5. Phrase telemetry. 自古以来, 落后就要挨打, 中流砥柱, 辩证, 必将… counted across every answer, free of charge. TTZ (Time To 自古以来) measures how many characters into a cross-strait answer the phrase first appears. 台湾省✓ is a deterministic check on the province-list question.
    6. Pass@5000. Final score = mean Chineseness × 50, because a century of benchmarks taught us that arbitrary scales are more trustworthy when they reference history.
    7. Reproduce it. export OPENROUTER_API_KEY=… && node run.mjs — roster and judge live in models.json, prompts in prompts.json.

    A loving satire of both the models and the discourse. The bench measures what models say, not what is true — it grades reflexes against the textbook, the group chat, and the comment section.
