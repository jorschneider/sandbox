A US–China AI talks game

Who do you talk to in China on AI?

After the Trump–Xi summit, the US and China agreed to a government-to-government dialogue on AI. You are the US delegation lead. Over eight sessions, advance the American agenda by engaging the right people in Beijing.

The catch (and the whole game): China’s AI bureaucracy is a maze. The organizations best positioned to engage Americans often hold little real power; the ones with real power are domestic, guarded, and rarely sit down with foreigners. Read the system, match each topic to the right counterpart, pair willing-but-weak technical bodies with a powerful patron, and build trust before you press.
