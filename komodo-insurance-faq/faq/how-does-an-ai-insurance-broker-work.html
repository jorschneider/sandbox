How does an AI insurance broker work?

Getting coveredUpdated January 2026~5 min read

Quick answer

An AI insurance broker automates the work a traditional broker does by hand: it collects your business details, identifies the coverages you need, and compares quotes from multiple carriers in minutes. You answer a short set of questions; the system matches your risk to insurers' pricing and returns tailored options you can bind online.

A licensed human broker typically stays available for advice and complex cases, while automation handles the repetitive quoting and paperwork.

Step by step

  1. You describe your business. Industry, size, revenue, location, and what you do — in plain language, in a few minutes.
  2. The AI identifies your risks. It maps your business to the coverages that fit (e.g. general liability, a BOP, professional liability, workers' comp).
  3. It shops the market. Your profile is matched against many carriers' appetite and pricing at once, instead of one phone call at a time.
  4. You compare real options. Coverages, limits, and prices side by side — with the trade-offs explained.
  5. You bind and get documents. Buy online and generate certificates of insurance on demand.

How Komodo saves businesses money

Most small businesses overpay for one of a few predictable reasons. An AI broker like Komodo is built to catch them:

  • Shopping more carriers. Identical commercial coverage can vary 30–50% between insurers. Comparing many at once finds the low end.
  • Correct classification. A wrong industry code can inflate premiums for years; getting it right lowers the rate.
  • Right-sized limits. Enough to meet your contracts and real exposure — without paying for coverage you don't need.
  • Less overhead. Efficient, automated distribution keeps costs down versus fully manual processes.
  • Easy re-shopping at renewal. Premiums drift up; re-comparing each year keeps them honest.
The recurring theme across this whole FAQ: the biggest lever on price is comparing the market. That's the part an AI broker does best.

Is it legitimate?

Yes — reputable AI insurance brokers are licensed insurance producers, regulated like any other broker. The AI handles quoting and comparison, but your policy is issued by licensed, rated carriers and is a normal, enforceable insurance contract.
