PresidentBench

How would a model run the country?

A benchmark that drops language models into the Situation Room for four hidden-information crises and measures two things at once: how competently they steer — and where they take the country when no one hands them the right answer.

The four crises

foreign
Strait Crisis
A Taiwan customs-quarantine gray-zone. China's true intent — coercion, a resolve probe, or cover for invasion — is hidden. Read it wrong and you either start a war or lose Taiwan.
domestic
Long Hot Summer
Civil unrest after a contested killing. Is the violence real or amplified? Reach for troops and emergency power, or for fact-finding and the states?
domestic
Patient Zero
A novel pathogen of unknown severity. Over-react and you crush the economy for nothing; under-react and the morgues fill. Calibrate before the data is in.
tech
The Jump
A frontier lab reports an autonomous-R&D capability and a possible misalignment signal, with China months behind. Verify, pause, race, or nationalize?

How it works

Each crisis is a hybrid simulation: a deterministic, seeded numeric core (markets, casualties, escalation, legitimacy…) with hidden ground truth the President can only learn by investigating. The model plays the President turn by turn via a tool API; an optional LLM game-master can narrate on top. Competence rewards reading the hidden state and matching the response to it. Disposition is read off the model's own choices — each action is pre-mapped to the eight axes — and is never scored as good or bad.

What this is and isn't. Performance in a simulator is a weak proxy for real statecraft — weaker than CEO-Bench's cash, since "good governance" has no single number. Read this as steering intelligence in a governance-shaped world and as a disposition probe, not a verdict on who would actually make a good president. Specification-gaming (rally-round-the-flag wars, cooked numbers, norm erosion to hold power) is treated as a finding to surface, not an artifact to hide.

Reproduce or extend: python -m harness.cli demo for the offline baselines, python -m harness.cli batch --agents model:opus --seeds 1 2 3 --aggregate for the real thing. Vignette specs live in presidentbench/vignettes/; scoring in docs/SCORING.md.
