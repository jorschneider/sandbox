    ⚠️ Please do not take this to your doctor

    This is a joke with footnotes. Observational studies report associations — people who do X tend to live longer — not years you can withdraw from an ATM. The effect sizes here are drawn from real, frequently-cited papers, then treated with the reckless literalism of a health-influencer thread.

    Adding them together is the entire gag. In reality the effects overlap, confound, cancel, and mostly reflect the sort of person who already flosses. Optimists don't live longer because they're optimistic; people who can afford saunas can afford a lot of things. A daily glass of wine "adding" years is the most famous correlation ever to be quietly walked back. You cannot out-habit your genes, your zip code, or a bus.
